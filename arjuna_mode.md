Current Problem Statement — Arjuna Mode

The current Arjuna Mode feels artificial, repetitive, and spiritually forced instead of emotionally natural and conversational.

Right now, the system behaves like a “Bhagavad Gita quote generator” rather than an emotionally intelligent companion.

Issues Happening Currently
1. Every Input Is Treated Negatively

Even normal or positive messages like:

“hello”
“hi”
“today was good”
casual conversation

are interpreted as emotional distress.

This causes the AI to:

unnecessarily become serious
assume sadness/anxiety
push spiritual guidance even when not needed

Result:
❌ emotionally inaccurate conversations

2. Forced Bhagavad Gita Injection

The AI currently inserts Bhagavad Gita verses in almost every response.

This creates:

repetitive interactions
unnatural flow
preachy behavior
emotional disconnect

Instead of feeling wise, it feels forced.

3. Repetitive Verse Usage

The system keeps returning:

same 2–3 shlokas
same philosophical ideas
same “karma/outcome” concepts

again and again.

This makes the experience:
❌ predictable
❌ boring
❌ shallow

4. No Real Conversation Building

Currently:

user speaks once
AI instantly gives wisdom

There is:
❌ no emotional buildup
❌ no trust-building
❌ no reflective questioning
❌ no conversational depth

Because of this, the wisdom does not feel earned.

5. Gita Is Surface-Level Instead of Hidden Depth

The app currently feels like:
❌ “scripture-first chatbot”

But the intended experience is:
✅ “human-first emotionally intelligent companion”

The Bhagavad Gita should act as:

subtle wisdom
contextual depth
occasional reflective insight

NOT as constant visible output.

Intended Experience

The desired Arjuna Mode experience should:

converse naturally first
understand emotional context
identify mood correctly
build conversation slowly
ask reflective questions
respond like a calm human
only introduce wisdom when emotionally relevant
introduce Gita subtly after a few meaningful exchanges
Desired Conversation Style

Instead of:

❌
User:

“hello”

AI:

“Bhagavad Gita Chapter 2 Verse 47 says…”

The desired interaction is:

✅
User:

“hello”

AI:

“Hey :)
How’s your mind feeling today?”

Then after meaningful conversation:

slight wisdom
gentle perspective
subtle Gita essence
Core Design Shift Needed

Current Architecture:

User Message
→ Immediate Gita Verse
→ Spiritual Explanation

Desired Architecture:

User Message
→ Mood Detection
→ Natural Conversation
→ Emotional Understanding
→ Reflection
→ Conditional Wisdom Injection
→ Optional Gita Insight
Final Goal

Arjuna Mode should feel like:
✅ a wise emotionally intelligent companion

NOT:
❌ a Bhagavad Gita search engine.
