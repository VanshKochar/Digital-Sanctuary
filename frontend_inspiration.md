Your app should not feel like a “mental health app.”
It should feel like a **digital sanctuary**.

Not an app.
An experience.

When someone opens it at 2 AM during anxiety, heartbreak, loneliness, overthinking, or burnout… the UI itself should calm them before the AI even speaks.

That is the level you should aim for.

---

# Core Design Philosophy

## Emotion You Want to Create

The moment the user opens the app:

* breathing slows down
* eyes relax
* mind feels safe
* no pressure
* no clutter
* no “tech company” vibe
* no dopamine overload
* feels sacred yet modern
* feels spiritual without being religious-heavy
* feels premium without being luxury-heavy
* feels alive but silent

Think:

* meditation retreat + Apple-level minimalism
* ancient wisdom + futuristic softness
* calm spirituality + award-winning interaction design

---

# The Core Design Language

## Design Style Name

You should internally call the style:

# “Sacred Minimalism”

A blend of:

* Japanese zen interfaces
* Indian spiritual aesthetics
* Apple Human Interface softness
* modern glassmorphism
* cinematic lighting
* organic motion

---

# COLOR SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)

Do NOT use bright spiritual colors.

Avoid:

* hard saffron
* bright yellow
* strong orange
* heavy gradients
* dark red

Your UI should feel:

* airy
* breathable
* divine
* emotionally warm

---
